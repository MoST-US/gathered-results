SCALE · replication artifact · additivity and burst factor

Can the capacity of LLM request mixes be predicted?

Analysis of 301 MoST mix runs (114 designs) and 360 pure-class runs on three 7–8 B models served on NVIDIA A40 GPUs. It tests whether SCALE's Capacity Units add up when request classes are mixed, and characterises the burst factor MIT / MST that governs burst allowances.

q = −0.96estimated combination exponent (harmonic = −1)
−5.5 %mean bias of mixes versus the harmonic law
294/301runs within the capacity range of their classes
B ≈ 3.5median burst factor, practically equal across models
